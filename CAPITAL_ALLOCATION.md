# CAPITAL ALLOCATION

This block defines how total capital is allocated across assets and trades.

Capital allocation is static by rules and adjusted ONLY during reviews.

---

## Objectives

- Prevent overexposure
- Limit correlation risk
- Ensure survivability

---

## Global Limits

- Max capital exposed at once: **30% of equity**
- Max risk at one time (sum of open risks): **3%**
- Max simultaneous positions: **3**

---

## Per-Asset Limits

- Single asset exposure: **≤ 15% of equity**
- Single asset risk at a time: **≤ 1%**

---

## Correlation Rules

- Highly correlated assets count as ONE exposure
- If correlation is unclear → treat as correlated

---

## Allocation Rules

- Allocation is decided BEFORE the session
- No reallocating during live trades
- No adding capital to losing positions

---

## Forbidden Actions

- Full capital deployment
- “Conviction-based” allocation
- Revenge allocation after loss

---

## Enforcement

- Allocation breach = trading halt
- Halt lasts until next review
